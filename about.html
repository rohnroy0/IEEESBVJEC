Who We Are

IEEE SB VJEC is a vibrant student branch at Vimal Jyothi Engineering College, committed to fostering technical innovation, collaboration, and leadership. We aim to provide a platform for students to excel in their fields, connect with like-minded peers, and grow into future leaders in technology and innovation.

Our Mission

To inspire students to develop their technical and leadership skills, enabling them to make impactful contributions to society through innovative solutions.

Our Vision

To become a leading IEEE Student Branch by cultivating a collaborative environment where students can thrive and excel in the fields of science and technology.
