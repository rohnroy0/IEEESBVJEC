Our Events

Discover the exciting events organized by IEEE SB VJEC.

visionX

VisionX 2025 is the flagship event by IEEE SB VJEC, celebrating innovation and creativity. Held on January 18 & 19 at Vimal Jyothi Engineering College, the event is a platform for students and professionals to explore new horizons of technology and art. It features hands-on workshops in web and graphic design, photography sessions, and a photo walk, offering participants a chance to refine their skills under expert guidance. Adding to the excitement are thrilling treks, a vibrant musical night, and an electrifying DJ experience, making VisionX not just an event but a celebration of talent, teamwork, and passion.

Aidex

AIDEX: AI Summit 2024 was a dynamic two-day event held at VJEC, organized by IEEE CIS SBC VJEC and IEEE SB VJEC. The summit celebrated the transformative power of artificial intelligence through captivating technical talks, hands-on workshops, a refreshing trek, a soulful musical evening, and an electrifying DJ night. AIDEX 2024 brought together technology enthusiasts, innovators, and creators, fostering an environment of learning, collaboration, and unforgettable experiences, making it a landmark event for the future of AI exploration.
