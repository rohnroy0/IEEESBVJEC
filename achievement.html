Our Achievements

IEEE SB VJEC has consistently excelled in various fields, showcasing exceptional skills and commitment. Here are some of our key achievements:

"IEEE PES Day 2024 - Celebrating Excellence"

Congratulations to Abhinav VM, MDC of IEEE SB VJEC, for being selected as the IEEE PES Day 2024 Brand Ambassador. This achievement highlights his dedication and leadership within the IEEE community, contributing to the promotion and success of IEEE PES initiatives.

IEEE LINK Leadership Achievement - District Coordinator 2024

Congratulations to Ms. Treesa Binoy (2022–26 Batch) on being selected as the District Coordinator for the IEEE LINK Student Leadership Team 2024, representing the districts of Kasaragod, Kannur, and Wayanad. This recognition reflects her leadership skills and dedication to advancing IEEE's vision at the regional level.

Tink Her Hack 2.0 - Empowering Women in Tech

TinkerHub and IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) at Vimal Jyothi Engineering College, Chemperi, successfully hosted Tink Her Hack 2.0 in 2024. This innovative hackathon provided a platform for women in tech to showcase their skills, collaborate, and create impactful solutions. The event exemplifies the commitment to fostering creativity and inclusion within the tech community.

IEEE WiE Volunteer Award!

Ms. Aleena K Shibu from the 2020–24 batch has been honored with the prestigious IEEE Kerala Section Outstanding WiE Student Volunteer Award 2023 (Special Mention). This award recognizes her exceptional dedication and contributions as a volunteer in the Women in Engineering (WiE) community. We are proud of her achievements and wish her continued success in all future endeavors!.

Heartfelt congratulations to Ujwel C, Secretary of the Computer Science Department at IEEE SB VJEC, for being selected as the IEEE Xtreme 17.0 Ambassador. This prestigious role highlights your dedication, leadership, and commitment to excellence. Your achievements continue to inspire and uplift the IEEE SB VJEC community. Best wishes as you represent us on this global platform and contribute to the success of IEEE Xtreme 17.0! Keep making us proud!
